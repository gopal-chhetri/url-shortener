What else it does

Recent links skip the database
Recently used links are cached in Redis, so redirecting them doesn't touch Postgres. 
GET   /k3Xa9
redis hit
302   → example.com/blog/…
Turn a link off without deleting it
A disabled link stops redirecting but keeps its click history, so you can turn it back on later.
Roles and an audit log
Admins manage every link and users manage their own. The audit log records each change and who made it.

Privacy

ShortURL stores what it needs to run links and analytics: your account, your links, and anonymous click data (browser, device, and coarse location when available). We don't sell personal data. Ask your administrator to delete your account.
